Dr. Amélie

Braider, instructor, doctor of philosophy. The woman who founded Queens Hairstyle.

Book an appointment
I

The braider

Knotless and box braids, cornrows and stitch braids, Senegalese and passion twists, micro-locs and locs: her menu covers the full vocabulary of African hairstyling.

Her photographs show its signature: clean geometric parts, heart and wave patterns, gold finishing touches.

II

The instructor

On Instagram she introduces herself as a Montréal braider and instructor. She teaches the craft she practises every day in the studio.

III

The scholar

The title before her name is earned: she holds a doctorate in philosophy.

A rare figure in her field: a scholar who works with her hands.
